## Overview

Retail businesses generate enormous volumes of data from customer interactions, transactions, inventory systems, and market trends. Transforming this data into actionable insights is one of the most impactful applications of Artificial Intelligence.

This repository showcases Machine Learning and Deep Learning projects designed to address practical challenges in retail and commerce through data-driven solutions. The collection demonstrates applications ranging from customer behavior analysis and demand forecasting to recommendation systems and predictive business intelligence.

The objective is to bridge the gap between theoretical machine learning concepts and their practical implementation in real-world business scenarios.
